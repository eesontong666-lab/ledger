装一个属于你自己的记账 App

付款之后，轻点 iPhone 背面两下，它就自动截图、读出金额和商家，帮你记一笔。

  • 完全免费，资料只属于你自己
  • 只用一个 6 位密码进入，不用邮箱登录
  • 外币会自动换成马币，认得常见商家

你需要：一台 iPhone，大约 10–15 分钟。全程照着这里一步一步点就可以，不用懂电脑。

💡 前面 3 步（注册和安装）用电脑做比较顺；只有手机也可以。后面的步骤要在 iPhone 上做。

Set up your own bookkeeping app

After you pay, double-tap the back of your iPhone. It takes a screenshot, reads the amount and merchant, and logs the expense for you.

  • Completely free, and the data is yours alone
  • One 6-digit passcode, no email login
  • Foreign currencies are converted to RM; common merchants are recognised

You need: an iPhone and about 10–15 minutes. Just follow each step here; no technical knowledge needed.

💡 The first 3 steps (sign-up and install) are smoother on a computer, but a phone works too. The later steps are done on your iPhone.
🐙

注册 GitHub（免费）

GitHub 用来存放你这一份 App 的代码。你之后不需要再打开它。

打开 GitHub 注册 ↗
  1. 填邮箱、设一个密码、取一个用户名。
  2. 到邮箱收验证码，填进去。
  3. 看到 GitHub 的首页就完成了，回到这一页。
已经有 GitHub 账号？直接点「下一步」。

Sign up for GitHub (free)

GitHub stores your copy of the app's code. You won't need to open it again afterwards.

Open GitHub sign-up ↗
  1. Enter your email, choose a password and a username.
  2. Check your email for the code and enter it.
  3. When you see the GitHub home page you are done. Come back here.
Already have a GitHub account? Just tap Next.
▲

注册 Vercel（免费）

Vercel 是让你的 App 在网上运作的地方。

打开 Vercel 注册 ↗
  1. 方案选 Hobby（免费的那个），填你的名字。
  2. 点 Continue with GitHub。
  3. GitHub 问你要不要授权，点绿色的 Authorize Vercel。
  4. 如果要你验证手机号码，照做就可以。
⚠️ 一定要选 Continue with GitHub，不要用邮箱注册，下一步才接得上。

Sign up for Vercel (free)

Vercel is where your app runs on the internet.

Open Vercel sign-up ↗
  1. Choose the Hobby plan (the free one) and enter your name.
  2. Tap Continue with GitHub.
  3. GitHub asks for permission: tap the green Authorize Vercel.
  4. If it asks to verify your phone number, do so.
⚠️ Be sure to choose Continue with GitHub, not email, or the next step will not connect.
🚀

安装你的 App

点下面的按钮，然后在打开的页面里照顺序做 3 件事。

开始安装 ↗
  1. Create Git Repository
    名字不用改，直接点 Create。如果它先要你安装 GitHub 的权限，点 Install，选 All repositories。
  2. Add Supabase（这是存账本的数据库）
    点 Add → 同意条款 → Region 选 Singapore → Plan 选 Free → 点 Create。
  3. Deploy
    点 Deploy，等大约 2 分钟，直到出现彩带和 Congratulations。
💡 这一页先不要关。安装好之后回来点「下一步」。

Install your app

Tap the button below, then do these 3 things in order on the page that opens.

Start install ↗
  1. Create Git Repository
    Keep the name and tap Create. If it first asks to install GitHub access, tap Install and choose All repositories.
  2. Add Supabase (the database that stores your ledger)
    Tap Add → accept the terms → Region Singapore → Plan Free → tap Create.
  3. Deploy
    Tap Deploy and wait about 2 minutes until you see confetti and Congratulations.
💡 Keep this page open. Come back and tap Next when the install has finished.
🔗

找到你的 App 网址

  1. 在 Congratulations 的画面，点 Continue to Dashboard。
  2. 页面上有一行 Domains，下面那个以 .vercel.app 结尾的就是你的网址，例如 my-ledger-abc.vercel.app。
  3. 把这个网址发到你自己的 WhatsApp 或备忘录，等下要在 iPhone 上打开。
⚠️ 这个网址就是你的账本入口，不要发给别人。

Find your app's address

  1. On the Congratulations screen, tap Continue to Dashboard.
  2. Look for Domains. The one ending in .vercel.app is your address, for example my-ledger-abc.vercel.app.
  3. Send that address to yourself (WhatsApp or Notes). You will open it on your iPhone next.
⚠️ This address is the door to your ledger. Do not share it.
🔒

设置你的 6 位密码

  1. 在 iPhone 上用 Safari 打开你的网址。
  2. 画面会出现数字键盘，写着「设置 6 位密码」。输入你想要的 6 个数字。
  3. 再输入一次确认，就会进到 App 的首页。
⚠️ 安装好之后请马上做这一步。第一个设密码的人就是这本账的主人。
以后每次打开 App 只需要这 6 个数字。连续输错 5 次会锁 15 分钟。

Set your 6-digit passcode

  1. On your iPhone, open your address in Safari.
  2. A number pad appears saying “设置 6 位密码” (set a 6-digit passcode). Enter the 6 digits you want.
  3. Enter them again to confirm. You land on the app's home screen.
⚠️ Do this right after installing. Whoever sets the passcode first owns the ledger.
From now on those 6 digits are all you need. Five wrong tries lock it for 15 minutes.

加到主屏幕

这样它就像一个普通的 App，有自己的图标。

  1. 在 Safari 里点底部中间的 分享 按钮（一个方框加向上的箭头）。
  2. 往下滑，点 添加到主屏幕（Add to Home Screen）。
  3. 点右上角的 添加。

回到主屏幕，会看到一个绿色的图标。以后从这里打开。

Add it to your Home Screen

It then behaves like a normal app with its own icon.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom centre (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add at the top right.

Back on your Home Screen you will see a green icon. Open the app from there from now on.

加上你的银行账户

  1. 打开 App，点底部第二个图标 👛 账户。
  2. 点 ＋ 添加账户。
  3. 填名称（例如 Maybank、日常开销）和现在的余额，点 保存。

有几个账户就加几个。投资和信用卡欠款也可以在同一个地方加。

💡 App 的界面是中文。看不懂的地方可以截图问把这个链接发给你的朋友。

Add your bank accounts

  1. Open the app and tap the second icon at the bottom, 👛 账户 (Accounts).
  2. Tap ＋ 添加账户 (Add account).
  3. Enter a name (for example Maybank, Daily Expenses) and the current balance, then tap 保存 (Save).

Add as many as you have. Investments and credit-card debt go in the same place.

💡 The app's interface is in Chinese. If something is unclear, send a screenshot to the friend who gave you this link.

开启「敲两下自动记账」

这一步请用 Safari 打开你的网址来做（不要用主屏幕的图标）。

  1. 点底部最右边的 ⚙️ 设置 → 📲 截图记账。右上角可以切换 中 / EN。
  2. ① 生成专属链接，然后点 复制。它只显示一次。
  3. ② 下载快捷指令 → 点 下载 → 点开下载好的文件 → 长按输入框 粘贴 → 添加快捷指令。
  4. ③ 到 iPhone 的 设置 → 辅助功能 → 触控 → 轻点背面 → 轻点两下，选 Ledger Screenshot。
英文系统的路径是：Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap。

Turn on “double-tap to log”

Do this step with your address open in Safari (not from the Home Screen icon).

  1. Tap ⚙️ 设置 (Settings) at the bottom right → 📲 截图记账. Switch to EN at the top right.
  2. ① Generate my link, then tap Copy. It is shown only once.
  3. ② Download Shortcut → tap Download → open the downloaded file → long-press the box and Paste → Add Shortcut.
  4. ③ On your iPhone go to Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap and choose Ledger Screenshot.

试一笔

  1. 打开银行 App 里任何一笔付款成功或交易详情的画面。
  2. 轻点手机背面两下。
  3. 第一次会问几次权限，都选 始终允许（Always Allow）。
  4. 几秒后会出现通知：✅ 已记账 RM8.00 · 商家。

如果它不确定这笔算哪一类，会弹出选单让你点「食 / 衣 / 住 / 行 / 其他」。

之后要知道的两件事
• 超过一星期没用，数据库会自动休眠。到 supabase.com/dashboard 登录，点 Restore 就恢复，资料不会丢。
• 忘记密码：在 Supabase 的 Table Editor 打开 app_passcode，把 passcode_hash 清空，再打开 App 就能重新设置。

Try one

  1. Open any payment-success or transaction-detail screen in your banking app.
  2. Double-tap the back of your phone.
  3. The first time it asks for permission a few times. Choose Always Allow.
  4. A few seconds later a notification appears: ✅ 已记账 RM8.00 · merchant.

If it is not sure about the category, a menu pops up: 食 food · 衣 shopping · 住 home · 行 transport · 其他 other.

Two things for later
• After about a week without use the database goes to sleep. Sign in at supabase.com/dashboard and tap Restore; nothing is lost.
• Forgot the passcode: in Supabase → Table Editor → app_passcode, clear passcode_hash, then open the app to set a new one.
